# Kept

A daily habit coach built on the ideas in Tom Corley's *Rich Habits*. Two minutes a day to learn a habit, act on it, and keep it.

## The problem

People read habit books, feel motivated, and a week later have changed nothing. Habit trackers let you tick a box but never teach you anything. Book apps teach you but never check whether you acted.

## How it works

On day 1 you turn a dream into a plan: a yearly goal, a monthly goal and a few daily habits.

Then, every day:

1. **Report back.** Did you do what you promised yesterday? Yes, Partly or No.
2. **Recall.** Explain one habit in your own words. The app checks your answer.
3. **Commit.** "After I [routine], I will [tiny action]". One click adds it to your calendar.

The app adapts as you go. Actions shrink after misses and grow after good runs, and habits you forget come back sooner. Your progress shows as a ledger of kept and slipped habits.
