# The Streak counts finished Check-ins, not kept Promises

A user's Streak grows on every Kept day they finish a Check-in, whatever their Report said, so a No counts as much as a Yes. Counting only Yes or Partly looks more motivating, but it gives people a reason to tap Yes to save the Streak, and a false Yes grows the next Promise a Size and makes the ledger untrue, which is the proof Kept shows. Duolingo's streak likewise counts doing a lesson, not doing it well.

## Considered Options

- **Yes or Partly days.** Rejected: rewards a false Report, as above.
- **Any Report.** Rejected: the Check-in after a gap has no Report to make (the open Promise has closed as Unreported), so the day someone comes back, the day Comeback exists for, wouldn't count.

## Consequences

- One Empty day is forgiven, and forgiven again only after 3 finished Check-ins in a row, matching the three-Yeses Size rule. A Streak that starts again after one ended earns its forgiveness the same way, so checking in every other day can't keep starting forgiven Streaks and collecting Comebacks. Lally et al. (2010) found one missed opportunity didn't measurably slow habit formation, and Silverman & Barasch (JCR 2023) found a broken streak hurt later engagement less when it could be repaired.
- An ended Streak is never shown as lost: the Ledger keeps a Best streak, and the next Check-in is a Comeback. Silverman & Barasch report that pointing out a broken streak typically doesn't work.
- The first finished Check-in after any Empty day is a Comeback and is counted, after the top intervention in Milkman et al.'s gym megastudy (Nature 2021), which rewarded returning after a missed workout.
- The Streak needs no table of its own: a committed Promise marks a finished Check-in, and the Events already record those (ADR 0001).
- "Still checking in" on the results page means submitting a Report, so today it misses Comeback days. After the Nov 5 demo it changes to finishing a Check-in, so the results page and the Streak count showing up the same way.
- A Comeback doesn't shrink the Promise. A gap still never counts against its Size, as the walkthrough says.
