❓ **Q2 - Is a Check-in one visit, or one Kept day's worth of steps?** Say Sara taps **Yes**, closes the tab mid-recall, and comes back an hour later. ➡️ **Yes: a Check-in is one Kept day's report, learning and commit, however many visits it takes.** Each step saves the moment it's done. Coming back resumes at the first unfinished step. The glossary entry changes from "a visit" to that.

---

❓ **Q3 - Does tapping a report answer move straight to Learn, with no confirm?** That's what the prototype does. A mis-tap gets fixed on the Done-for-today screen, where the report can be changed until 4am.

➡️ **Yes, move straight on.** One tap is the whole point of a 2-minute loop, and the fix is already there.

❓ **Q4 - Is there no going back to an earlier step?** In B, should Sara be able to go from Promise back to Learn, or from Learn back to Report?

➡️ **Yes, no back button.** A recall answer is graded and saved, so redoing it would muddy the day 1 vs day 5 recall comparison. The report is changed on the Done screen instead. Browser back just reloads `/today` at the current step.

➡️ **Yes.** If the Learn card fails to load, Act records the error with `captureError`, skips the step, and goes on to Promise. Keeping promises is the habit; losing a day of learning is cheaper than losing the loop.
