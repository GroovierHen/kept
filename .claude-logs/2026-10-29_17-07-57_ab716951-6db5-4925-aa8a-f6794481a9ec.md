## What Kept is
A 2-minute-a-day money-habit coach based on Corley's *Rich Habits*. It checks whether people actually did what they said they'd do, and it teaches the money skill behind each habit using their own numbers.

- **Day 1:** sign up, then turn a dream into a plan (Claude does the plan and the user edits it). The user gets their first habit and skill, then picks a promise from a list ranked by pounds saved, and it goes into their calendar as an `.ics` reminder.
- **Every day after:** report back (Yes, Partly or No), answer a recall question (code works out the right answer, Claude marks it 0–2, and it falls back to multiple choice if Claude is down), learn a new habit, make a new promise.
- **Engines:** promise coach (ranks promises by £), adaptive ladder (small, medium and large actions), review scheduler (1, 3 and 7 days), and a ledger built from an event log.
- **AI in only three places:** making the plan, marking answers, and "Help me choose". All the maths and all the text is ours.
- **Deadlines:** the must tier is due **Fri Oct 30**, features freeze Tue Nov 3, and the final demo is Thu Nov 5. Testers need to start by Oct 30.
