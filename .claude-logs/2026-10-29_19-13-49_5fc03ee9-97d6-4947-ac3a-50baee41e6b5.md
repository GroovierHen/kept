**Kept** is a daily money-habit coach based on Tom Corley's *Rich Habits*. You and Abdelmalek Maskri are building it for 8x Playmakers C2, which runs Oct 28 – Nov 6, 2026.

**The problem it solves:** people read a habit book, feel motivated, and a week later nothing has changed. Habit trackers let you tick boxes but don't teach you anything. Book apps teach but never check whether you acted. Kept does both in about two minutes a day.

**How it works**
- **Day 1:** you turn a dream into a plan: a £ goal for the year, broken down into a monthly goal and a few daily "promises". The promises are ranked by how much £ they save, and code works out those numbers, not the AI.
- **Every day:**
  1. **Report back:** did you keep yesterday's promise? Yes, Partly or No.
  2. **Recall:** explain one habit in your own words. Claude grades it 0–2 against an answer the code works out, and falls back to multiple choice if needed.
  3. **Commit:** "After I [routine], I will [tiny action]". One click adds it to your calendar as an .ics file.
- **It adapts:** actions get smaller after misses and bigger after good runs (a small/medium/large ladder). Habits you forget come back sooner, with reviews 1, 3 and 7 days later. Your progress is an event-sourced ledger of kept and slipped promises.

**Key rules from the spec:** AI is used in only three places: the plan, grading, and "Help me choose". All maths and copy come from code or our own text. It's education, not financial advice. It never promises riches, and it credits Corley.

**Who owns what** ([docs/product/tasks.md](docs/product/tasks.md))
- **You (Talha), the "Act" half:** login and DB tables, the daily loop screen, the promise coach, the adaptive engine, .ics reminders, the ledger and projections, and the 4 shared design components.
- **Abdelmalek, the "Learn" half:** habit content, money maths, the recall grader, the review scheduler and the goal planner. Your screens call his functions: `getTodaysLesson`, `gradeRecall` and `getDueReviews`.

**Stack:** it uses the 8xsocial template-web stack, which you chose over the proposal's SQLite and ngrok setup: Next.js 16, React 19, Prisma, Supabase Postgres and Auth, deployed on Vercel.

**Where it stands:** it's still basically the template. The pages are the template's own (`admin`, `analytics-demo`) and the Prisma schema has only template models, so no Kept tables or screens exist yet.

**Deadlines:** the must-have tier is due **tomorrow (Fri Oct 30)**. For you that means login, DB, the shared design pieces, the daily loop screens and the promise suggestions. Testers start Oct 31, feature freeze is Nov 3, and the final demo is Nov 5.
