    - a new user's time zone is stored
    - a later sign-in leaves it unchanged
    - invalid or missing zones fall back
    - a failed Event rolls the user back
    - concurrent first sign-ins record one Event
- **Browser:** the sign-up page's hidden field held `Asia/Karachi` with no console errors.
- **Not tested:** a full sign-up through Supabase, because `.env.local` points at the real project. That is the job of the #15 browser test. The database checks were a scratch script and aren't committed, as the spec adds no database test layer this week.

**Problems to know about**
1. **Merging puts every account behind the placeholders:** Today redirects to setup, so anyone signing in on production sees placeholder screens until the real pieces land. Progress sits in a per-device cookie for now, so a new device starts again at step 1. Only the `setup_finished` Event is saved in the database.
2. **Clicking through placeholders counts as Started:** the last step saves a real `setup_finished` Event, and those rows stay after the placeholders are gone. Delete any from non-team accounts before testers sign up on Saturday.
3. **A Gemini outage can trap a user:** the new walkthrough lets them type a first promise and get their plan on the next visit. As built, promise and reminder can't be reached without a confirmed plan, and Today sends them back to the plan step for as long as Gemini is down. This has to be settled with [#30](https://github.com/GroovierHen/kept/issues/30) and [#16](https://github.com/GroovierHen/kept/issues/16), because the fix depends on how they handle that failure.
4. **Abdelmalek needs to confirm `goal_saved`:** it has to mean all the goal screens are answered. If it's set after only the goal, returning users skip the two questions. I noted this in the code, but [#29](https://github.com/GroovierHen/kept/issues/29) doesn't say it yet.
