I found the cause. The AI isn't the problem. The plan is generated fine and then lost when it's being saved.

**What's happening**
- Your last four attempts all show as "ok" in `ai_usage`, about 11s each, with no retry. So OpenRouter, the key (valid until Nov 9) and GPT-6 Luna are all fine.
- `.env.local` points at the Supabase database in us-east-1. From your machine, one query takes about **1.1s** (I timed it).
- `savePlan` writes the goal, plan, milestones, actions, key ideas and the `plan_generated` event in one transaction. That's about 10 queries, so roughly 10s. Prisma kills any transaction that runs longer than **5s** and rolls it back. The action catches the error and shows "We couldn't make your plan right now". That's why the POST took 20s: about 10s for the AI and about 10s for the save that failed.
- Production runs right next to the database, so it isn't affected. Only local development is this slow.

**The fix** ([client.ts](src/server/db/client.ts))
Outside production, the Prisma client now allows a transaction to run for 30s and wait 10s to start. Production keeps Prisma's defaults (5s and 2s). This one setting covers all 14 transactions in the app, so other slow local saves like confirming a plan or a promise won't fail the same way. With the new limits, my test transaction of 12 queries finished in 5.6s. Before, it was killed at 5.3s. Typecheck, lint and Prettier pass.

**To try it:** restart `pnpm dev`. Hot reload keeps the old database client in memory, so the new limits only take effect after a restart. Then press the button again. Your answers are still saved, and the failed build was released, so you won't be blocked from retrying.
