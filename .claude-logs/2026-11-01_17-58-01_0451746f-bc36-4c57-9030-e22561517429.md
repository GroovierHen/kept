**Your site isn't getting lots of traffic, despite what "Your site is growing!" suggests.** The storage is being filled by how often you deploy, and each deployment is fairly large.

## What Function Storage is
Every time you push code, Vercel builds a new deployment and keeps a copy of its server code (the "function bundle"). This lets you open old previews or roll back. Function Storage is the total size of all those saved copies, and your free plan includes 10 GB.

## Why it reached 75% (about 7.5 GB)
- **Many deployments:** Kept has 58 saved deployments, all from Oct 30 to Nov 1: 39 previews and 19 production. A new one is created for pushes to a PR and for merges to `main`.
- **A large bundle:** Vercel shows about 53 MB per deployment for Kept. Your other project, `stockroom`, is under 1 MB per deployment across 14 deployments, so it barely counts.
- **What's taking the space:** I measured a local build, which came to about 112 MB before compression:

| Package | Size |
|---|---|
| Prisma (`@prisma/client` + `.prisma`) | about 82 MB |
| `sharp` image library | about 18 MB |
| Your own app code | under 5 MB |

The numbers fit, but this is my estimate: 7.5 GB across 58 deployments is about 129 MB each, close to that uncompressed size. That only works if Vercel counts bundles uncompressed, which its docs don't confirm. You can check under **Usage → Deployment Storage → Functions Storage** in the Vercel dashboard.

## Does it matter?
- **Cost:** the email is about free plan usage, so nothing here suggests you'll be charged.
- **Timing:** you've been creating about 20–25 deployments a day, so you could reach 10 GB in about a day.
- **What happens at 10 GB:** Vercel says older deployments get deleted straight away, while your current production deployment is kept. Its pricing page also says free projects can be paused over the limit, and some forum users report blocked deployments. That would be bad timing before a demo or feedback session.

## How to fix it
1. **Delete old preview deployments (my recommendation):** usage drops as soon as they're deleted, and your live site isn't affected. Removing most of the 39 previews should bring you down to about 2.5–3 GB. You'd lose those old preview links.
2. **Shorten retention:** in the project's **Settings → Security**, reduce how long preview deployments are kept, so this doesn't build up again.
3. **Shrink the bundle:** trimming Prisma is the biggest possible saving, but it needs a code change and testing, so I'd only do it if the first two aren't enough.
