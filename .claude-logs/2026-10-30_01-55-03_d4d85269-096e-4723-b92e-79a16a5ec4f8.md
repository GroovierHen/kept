I've built the landing page, sign-up page and sign-in page. Typecheck, lint on the app code, all 205 tests and the production build pass. Nothing is committed yet.

**The look.** The design skill offered three directions and I picked "the sentence is the layout": the hero says "Promises, kept." split in two, with a reel of the Today screen running between the halves. As you scroll, the reel steps through a check-in: report → recall → promise → ledger. The style is bottle green and gold with one typeface, Hanken Grotesk. Green and gold suits Corley's ledger books.

**The landing page**, top to bottom:
- **Hero:** the split sentence and the reel.
- **Two minutes a day:** a bar where each stage is as wide as the time it takes.
- **Type a goal:** the interactive part. Typing "£20,000 in 5 years" shows £4,000 this year, £335 a month, £77 a week and £11 a day, plus about £22,200 at an example 4%. A unit test checks these match Sara's numbers in the walkthrough.
- **Where does your money go?:** Sara's spends as switches, each showing what the promise against it is worth.
- **Corley section:** two public-domain ledgers from the Met, credited in the footer.
- **FAQ, closing and footer:** the FAQ has all answers open. The closing line "Sign me up as ___ and start." passes the email to the sign-up form through a short-lived cookie, not the URL.

**Sign-up, sign-in and auth:**
- Supabase email and password through server actions in [auth.ts](src/server/actions/auth.ts). A `User` row is created with `provisionUser`, and the errors are written for people to read.
- [/today](src/app/[locale]/today/page.tsx) is only a placeholder with a sign-out button, so the redirect after sign-in has somewhere to land.
- In the browser I checked a wrong password against Supabase (the error shows and the email stays filled) and the closing-line handoff (the sign-up form opens with the email in it).
- I haven't created a real account, so sign-up success → /today → sign-out is untested. For sign-up to log people straight in, "Confirm email" must be off in Supabase. If it's on, the form tells them to check their inbox.
